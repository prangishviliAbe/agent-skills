# The Slop Catalog: AI Clichés & High-Craft Replacements

Read when: You are diagnosing generic visual patterns and replacing them with bespoke, memorable design choices.

---

## 1. Hero Sections & Layout

| Generic AI Slop Cliché | High-Craft Bespoke Replacement |
| --- | --- |
| Centered headline + subtitle + 2 rounded pill buttons floating over a fuzzy purple radial glow. | Asymmetric editorial layout with left-aligned typographic anchor, live interactive product preview on the right, and concrete proof metrics. |
| Full-screen stock illustration of floating 3D spheres, isometric robots, or glowing glowing interconnected wire cubes. | High-fidelity interactive component preview, authentic high-res UI capture, or custom architectural schematic diagram. |
| Massive empty padding with no clear visual focal point. | Tight, confident rhythm with high information density, clear visual hierarchy, and purposeful whitespace. |

## 2. Features & Component Cards

| Generic AI Slop Cliché | High-Craft Bespoke Replacement |
| --- | --- |
| 3 identical cards in a row, each featuring a pastel circular background with a Lucide icon and 2 lines of generic text. | Asymmetrical Bento Grid where the flagship feature occupies a $2 \times 2$ span with live interactive controls, while secondary items show real telemetry data. |
| Flat cards with uniform 1px solid gray borders (`#e5e7eb` or `#27272a`). | Layered cards with subtle top-lit specular highlights (`inset 0 1px 0 oklch(1 0 0 / 0.15)`), dynamic hover spotlight borders, and backdrop blur. |
| Cards with no hover state or only a generic `opacity-80` transition. | Tactile micro-elevation: 3D perspective tilt, cursor-guided gradient illumination, and smooth spring physics. |

## 3. Typography & Microcopy

| Generic AI Slop Cliché | High-Craft Bespoke Replacement |
| --- | --- |
| "Transform your workflow with the power of Next-Gen AI" | "Cut p99 database query latency from 320ms to 14ms in three lines of configuration." |
| Inter or Roboto used for every single element across the entire page. | Expressive display typeface (editorial serif or bold technical grotesque) paired with a clean, highly legible body sans-serif. |
| Fake customer reviews with stock avatar portraits and generic praises ("Changed our business overnight!"). | Real customer logos, verbatim engineering quotes, and verifiable case-study metrics. |
